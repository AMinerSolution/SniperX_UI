# DPX product facts (EDIT ME - the swarm may only claim what is written here)

- Demand Planner X (DPX) is a Shopify app that forecasts demand per SKU and recommends what to reorder and when.
- It is built for ecommerce operations teams that currently plan inventory in spreadsheets.
- Goal: fewer stockouts and less overstock/dead stock.
- Install is through the Shopify App Store; it reads the store's own sales and inventory data.
- Founder-led company (SupliiChain).
